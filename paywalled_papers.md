## What this changes in the report

**Nothing yet — and that is deliberate.** Every Category A and B evidence quotation in the report came from full text actually retrieved. Papers in this file carry *no* quoted claims: they are marked "full text not retrievable" and were never assigned a future-work category by inference.

Once you have them, the useful follow-up is:

1. Re-check **R4, R5, R6, R11, R14** for explicit graph/GNN future-work sentences. Any hit moves them from Category C to A or B and strengthens Section 18.
2. Extract exact reported accuracies **and the validation protocol** from **F2, F3, F4, R14** to fill the "Not verified" cells in the Section 17 SOTA table.
3. Confirm whether **F3** (Klepl 2022) used subject-level splitting — this determines whether its numbers are comparable to ours at all, and it is the single most important unknown in the SOTA table.
