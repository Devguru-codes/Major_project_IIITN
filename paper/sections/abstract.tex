\textit{Objective.} EEG classification of Alzheimer's disease (AD) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD) on one
public dataset reports accuracies from 80\% to 99\%, but studies change representation, graph, model and
evaluation protocol at once. We isolated representation and graph construction under a fixed graph
neural network, and tested a candidate EEG marker linked to blood--brain-barrier dysfunction.
\textit{Approach.} On OpenNeuro ds004504 (36 AD, 23 FTD, 29 controls) we crossed seven node-feature
representations with three graph constructions under one fixed graph convolutional network, on 25
frozen subject-level splits (\nMainRuns{} runs) and 25 confirmation splits, with a by-subject mixed
model, equivalence tests, matched non-graph and deep-learning baselines, 16 ablations, permutation and
leakage experiments, and external validation on a Parkinson's disease cohort (ds004584, $n=149$). We
also characterised paroxysmal slow-wave events (PSWEs).
\textit{Main results.} Representation dominated ($F(\mixSelRepDf,\mixSelRepDenDf)=\mixSelRepF$; \mixConfRepF{}
on confirmation splits; \mixExtRepF{} externally), whereas the graph constructions were equivalent within
$\pm\eqMargin$ macro-F1. The best configuration (wavelet features, spatial graph) reached macro-F1
\confFOne{} (95\% CI \confFOneCiLo{}--\confFOneCiHi{}; permutation $p=\cpermPThreespatialP$); non-graph and
deep-learning baselines did not differ significantly, though differences of about \eqGvcMde{} could not be
excluded. Window-level splitting raised macro-F1 to \cleakPThreespatialLeakySubj{}. The representation
ranking did not transfer to Parkinson's disease ($\rho=\extRepsRho$). PSWEs were more frequent in AD (rate
ratio \psweAdjADRR{}) and FTD (\psweAdjFTDRR{}) but not in Parkinson's disease, tracked background slowing
($\rho=\psweSlowRho$), and showed no excess in patients whose slowing overlapped with controls, robustly
to the event rule, spectral resolution and artefacts.
\textit{Significance.} The evaluation protocol and the signal representation, not the graph construction,
determine reported performance, and the best representation is disease-specific. Fixed-threshold PSWEs on
scalp EEG mainly index background slowing. Code, frozen folds and all raw runs are released.
