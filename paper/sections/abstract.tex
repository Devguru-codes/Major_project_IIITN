\textit{Objective.} EEG classification of Alzheimer's disease (AD) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD) on one
public dataset reports accuracies from about 50\% to 99\%, but studies change representation, graph, model and
evaluation protocol at once. We isolated representation and graph construction under a fixed graph neural
network, and tested a blood--brain-barrier-linked EEG marker.
\textit{Approach.} On OpenNeuro ds004504 (36 AD, 23 FTD, 29 controls) we crossed seven node-feature
representations with three graph constructions under one compact, untuned graph convolutional network, on 25
frozen subject-level splits and 25 confirmation splits, with matched baselines, 16 ablations, leakage and
permutation experiments, external validation on a Parkinson's disease cohort ($n=149$), and analysis of
paroxysmal slow-wave events (PSWEs).
\textit{Main results.} Representation had the largest effect in every analysis and its ordering replicated
across fold sets ($\rho=\confRankRho$). In the planned split-level analysis, adjusted for overlapping training
sets, it was significant externally ($p\nbExtRepP$) but not on ds004504 ($p\nbSelRepP$, $p\nbConfRepP$); with
subjects as units it was significant everywhere. Graph construction had a detectable but negligible effect,
averaged graph types were equivalent within $\pm\eqMargin$ macro-F1, and a learned graph did no better. The
best configuration (wavelet features, spatial graph) reached macro-F1 \confFOne{} (95\% CI
\confFOneCiLo{}--\confFOneCiHi{}); non-graph and deep-learning baselines did not differ significantly.
Window-level splitting raised macro-F1 to \cleakPThreespatialLeakySubj{}. PSWEs were more frequent in AD (rate ratio \psweAdjADRR{}) and FTD
(\psweAdjFTDRR{}) but not in Parkinson's disease; the excess tracked background slowing
($\rho=\psweSlowRho$) and was not detectable among patients whose slowing overlapped with controls'.
\textit{Significance.} The evaluation protocol moves reported performance far more than any modelling choice
we tested, and representation matters more than graph construction. Fixed-threshold PSWEs on scalp EEG
mainly index background slowing. Code, folds and all raw runs are released.
